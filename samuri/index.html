How it works

From one line to a final cut.

  1. 一

    Write

    Tell Samuri the story. It plans the shots and writes prompts sized to pass each model's limits.

  2. 二

    Shoot

    Each shot is generated, with the last frame of one carried into the next so scenes connect.

  3. 三

    Check

    Every take is reviewed. Anything off gets fixed or reshot before you ever see it.

  4. 四

    Cut

    Shots are joined, sized to match, and set to your music. One file, ready to post.

What it checks

Every take, checked.

AI clips come back with small problems that wreck a finished video. Samuri catches them so you don't spend hours fixing them by hand.

  • Rejected prompts. Rewritten to fit length limits and filters, so they go through the first time.
  • Mismatched sizes and black bars. Every shot cropped and scaled to the same frame.
  • Characters that drift. Same fur, same armor, same face from the first shot to the last.
  • Takes that miss the brief. If the camera moved when it shouldn't, or the wind isn't blowing, it gets reshot.
  • Music that doesn't fit. Trimmed, faded, and laid under the scene's own sound.
