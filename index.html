How a claim works

Three steps from rug to refund

Coverage is set when a coin launches and can't be removed later, so buyers know exactly what they're getting.

01

Creator files a coin

Pick a name, an image and 1–5% coverage. Launches on a pump.fun-style bonding curve.

02

The coverage % of each buy and sell goes into that coin's own insurance pool.

Questions

Before you file

Who gets paid from the insurance pool?

Holders of an insured coin who are currently down on it. Each payout stops at break-even, and earlier payouts for the same coin are subtracted, so nobody gets paid twice for one loss.

Can someone fake a loss to get paid?

Tokens a wallet received by transfer count as free, so moving tokens between wallets doesn't create a loss. Payouts can also require a minimum holding time and a minimum loss.

Where does each trading fee go?

A platform fee, a share that buys back and burns $CLAIM, the coin's insurance %, and Meteora's protocol cut. The exact split for your coverage level shows on the filing page before you launch.

What happens when a coin graduates?

When the bonding curve fills, the coin moves to a Meteora pool and trades on Jupiter from then on, just like a pump.fun coin graduating.

Can coverage be changed after launch?

No. The coverage level is locked in when the coin is filed, so holders always know what they bought into.

Setup · fee configs

Creates one on-chain fee config per insurance level. Every coin launched on your site uses one of them, which is what routes your fee to you. You only do this once (it costs about 0.02 SOL per config).

Defaults match pump.fun's curve: coins start around 23.5 SOL market cap and graduate to a Meteora pool at about 406 SOL. Creation fee: 0 = free like pump.fun (if set, it must be at least 0.001 SOL).

Buy back & burn

        
Spends SOL from your wallet to buy your main coin on Jupiter, then burns exactly what was bought. Burned tokens are gone forever, which shrinks supply. Each claim adds the buyback share (0.25% of every trade, set by SITE.BUYBACK_PCT) to the amount below. The bot does this automatically every hour.
