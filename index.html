FAQ

Questions holders ask

How often does someone win?

Every hour, on the hour, 24 times a day. One eligible holder wins a random Rung 1 starter Limited.

How does trading up work?

Once a day the trade-up window opens. Each winner can keep their Limited or trade it for a chance at the next rung. If it hits, you get a random item from that rung and can go again next window. If it misses, you get Robux worth half your item. If you do nothing for 24 hours, you keep what you have.

When does the trade-up window open?

Every day at 01:00 UTC, which is 6 PM in Arizona. The timer at the top shows it in your own time zone.

How do I get my item?

Link your Roblox username by signing a message with your wallet, then join the Trade Up Roblox group. Limiteds are sent as a trade within 24 hours, and Robux goes out as a group payout.

Are the odds real?

Yes. The ladder on this page is exactly what the rolls use. Every roll is seeded by a Solana blockhash, and the script is open source.

Is this part of Roblox?

No. Trade Up is an independent community project. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Roblox Corporation. Roblox, Robux, and Limiteds belong to their owners.
