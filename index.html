Fee flow

Where every trade fee goes

This loop runs four times a day. Nothing leaves the vault except rewards paid to holders.

  1. 1 · Trade

    Someone buys or sells

    Every $ROVAULT trade pays a creator fee in SOL to the vault wallet.

  2. 2 · Collect

    Fees land on-chain

    The vault wallet is public, so anyone can watch its balance on Solscan.

  3. 3 · Convert

    SOL becomes Limiteds + R$

    60% buys Limiteds at or below their Rolimons value. 30% goes to the Robux pool, and 10% covers costs.

  4. 4 · Snapshot

    Holders get counted

    Each round snapshots at a random slot inside its 6-hour window, so buying right before the timer hits zero won't get you in.

  5. 5 · Distribute

    Draw every 6 hours

    One holder wins a Limited, and the round's Robux is split by weight. Results are posted with proof.

Fair split

Bigger bags earn more, but whales can't take it all

Your weight is the square root of your balance. If you hold 4× more tokens, you get 2× the weight. That keeps smaller holders in the running every round.

Eligible
Hold 100,000+ $ROVAULT at the snapshot, with your Roblox username linked.
Weight
√ balance. So 1M tokens gives 1,000 weight, and 4M tokens gives 2,000 weight.
Robux
Each round's Robux drip is split by weight across all eligible holders and paid through a Roblox group payout.
Limiteds
One Limited is drawn every 6 hours, with each wallet's odds set by its weight. A winner sits out the next 4 rounds (24 hours) so the same wallet can't sweep the vault.
Proof
Each draw is seeded with the Solana blockhash of its snapshot slot, so anyone can re-run it and get the same winner.
Excluded
The dev wallet, the bonding curve, LP wallets, and exchange wallets never enter the draw.

FAQ

Questions holders ask

How do I receive a Limited or Robux?

First, link your Roblox username by signing a message with your Solana wallet, and join the RoVault Roblox group. Robux is sent as a group payout after each round. If you win a Limited, the vault account sends you a trade request within 24 hours of the draw.

Why square-root weighting?

With a straight pro-rata split, one big wallet could win nearly every item. With √ balance, a wallet holding 100× more gets 10× the weight. Bigger holders still earn more, and smaller holders still have a real shot every round.

How are items priced?

The vault only buys at or below an item's Rolimons value, and it publishes what it paid. The vault totals on this page use Rolimons values, which can change.

Can I verify the draw?

Yes. After every round we publish the snapshot file, the slot number, and the blockhash. The draw script is open source, so you can run it yourself and get the same winners.

When are the draws?

Draws run every 6 hours at 00:00, 06:00, 12:00 and 18:00 UTC. In Arizona that's 5 PM, 11 PM, 5 AM and 11 AM. The timer at the top always shows the next one in your own time zone.

What if fees are small?

If fees can't restock the vault, the round still draws from items already held and the Robux drip still pays out. The vault never promises items it doesn't actually hold.

Is this affiliated with Roblox?

No. RoVault is an independent community project. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Roblox Corporation. Roblox, Robux, and Limiteds belong to their owners.
