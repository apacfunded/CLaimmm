Fee flow

Where every fee goes

This loop runs four times a day. Nothing leaves the stash except rewards for holders.

  1. 1 · Trade

    Someone buys or sells

    Every $STASH trade pays a creator fee in SOL to the stash wallet.

  2. 2 · Collect

    Fees land on-chain

    The stash wallet is public, so anyone can watch it fill up on Solscan.

  3. 3 · Shop

    SOL turns into loot

    60% buys Limiteds at or under Rolimons value, 30% tops up Robux, and 10% covers costs.

  4. 4 · Snapshot

    Holders get counted

    Each round snapshots at a random moment, so last-second buys don't count.

  5. 5 · Open

    The stash pays out

    One holder wins a Limited and Robux gets split by weight. Proof gets posted every round.

Fair split

Bigger bags win more. Whales can't win it all.

Your weight is the square root of your balance. If you hold 4× the tokens, you get 2× the weight.

Enter
Hold 100,000+ $STASH at the snapshot with your Roblox linked.
Weight
√ balance. So 1M tokens gives 1,000 weight, and 4M gives 2,000.
Robux
Each round's Robux is split by weight and sent as a Roblox group payout.
Limiteds
One Limited every 6 hours. A winner sits out the next 4 rounds, so nobody sweeps the stash.
Proof
Each draw is seeded by a Solana blockhash, so anyone can re-run it and get the same winner.
Excluded
Dev, bonding curve, LP, and exchange wallets never enter.

FAQ

Questions holders ask

How do I get my Limited or Robux?

Link your Roblox username by signing a message with your wallet, then join the Stash Roblox group. Robux goes out as a group payout after each round. If you win a Limited, you get a trade request within 24 hours.

When are the draws?

Every 6 hours, at 00:00, 06:00, 12:00 and 18:00 UTC. In Arizona that's 5 PM, 11 PM, 5 AM and 11 AM. The timer at the top shows the next one in your time zone.

Why square-root weighting?

With a straight split, one big wallet could win almost everything. With √ balance, holding 100× more gives you 10× the weight. Bigger holders still earn more, and small holders still have a real shot.

Can I check the draw myself?

Yes. Every round we post the snapshot, slot number, and blockhash. The draw script is open source, so anyone can run it and get the same winner.

What if fees are low?

The round still draws from items already in the stash, and the Robux drip still pays out. The stash never promises items it doesn't actually hold.

Is this part of Roblox?

No. Stash is an independent community project. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Roblox Corporation. Roblox, Robux, and Limiteds belong to their owners.
