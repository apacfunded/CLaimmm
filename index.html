FAQ

Questions holders ask

How often does the crate open?

Every hour, on the hour, 24 times a day. The timer at the top counts down to the next one in your time zone.

How do I get my Limited or Robux?

Link your Roblox username by signing a message with your wallet, then join the RoCrate Roblox group. Limiteds are sent as a trade within 24 hours, and Robux goes out as a group payout.

Are the odds real?

Yes. The loot table on this page is exactly what the hourly roll uses. Each roll is seeded by a Solana blockhash and the script is open source, so anyone can re-run it and get the same result.

What if the crate rolls an item it doesn't have?

You get the next item down in the same tier, or Robux of equal value. The crate never promises loot it can't deliver.

Why square-root weighting?

With a straight split, one big wallet would win almost every hour. With √ balance, holding 100× more gives you 10× the odds, so small holders still get real chances.

Is this part of Roblox?

No. RoCrate is an independent community project. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Roblox Corporation. Roblox, Robux, and Limiteds belong to their owners.
